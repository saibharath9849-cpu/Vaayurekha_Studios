About Vaayurekha Studios

We transform memories and emotions into handcrafted artworks that feel intimate, timeless and deeply personal.

Every composition is designed in our boutique studio with premium materials, soft gold detailing and thoughtful craftsmanship. We celebrate the beauty of pencil sketching, glass painting, devotional artistry, wedding keepsakes and personalized gifts made to become treasured heirlooms.
